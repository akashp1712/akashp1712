## What I work on

At **Salesforce** I build the agent runtime under Marketing Cloud's Content Studio: per-thread state, observational memory, skills, tools, image generation, and human approval points.

Independently I design and ship agent products. They run on a low-touch cadence. That is how I keep several systems in production without treating any of them as abandoned.

Before Salesforce: SMS platforms serving billions of messages, app-certification systems, home-network security. Nine-plus years across **Salesforce, Amazon, and McAfee**.
